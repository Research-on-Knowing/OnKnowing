#### About Insights on Knowing

Insights on Knowing is the publication of [Research on Knowing LLC](https://www.researchonknowing.com/). Big shifts often begin with a single focused idea, and this is where we share ours: short stories, sharp observations, and practical wisdom designed to make the unseen mechanics of work and life visible.

Research on Knowing puts practical epistemology to work. Epistemology is the theory of knowledge, "especially with regard to its methods, validity, and scope" (OxfordLanguages). Just as the telescope opened the stars and the microscope unlocked invisible worlds, new tools, software, and shifted perspectives make the hidden visible. We turn abstract theories of knowledge into actionable ways to learn, reason, and build.

Each entry stands on its own, and together they build, block by block, a clearer and more intentional way to learn, reason, and build.
